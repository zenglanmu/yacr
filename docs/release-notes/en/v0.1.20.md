# yacr v0.1.20

Source: `CI build layers` run
[45832528013](https://github.com/zenglanmu/yacr/actions/runs/45832528013) (tag `v0.1.20`); the assets are
attached by `.github/workflows/publish-release.yml`.

Released: 2026-11-26

## What changed

### Mesh modelling

- **Imported SketchUp meshes keep their source face identity** (`skp:face:*`, the source-face index): triangles that
  share a source face can be selected, moved and press-pulled as a face region instead of the
  whole body; meshes without face identity are refused with `mesh.face_identity_unavailable`.
- **`UNION` / `SUBTRACT` / `INTERSECT` now accept mesh bodies** (`AcDbSubDMesh`): the pure-Rust
  `manifold-rust` kernel runs on a background worker, the result must be closed, and it is written
  back as one mesh row, in one transaction, as one undo step. Exact solids keep the exact kernel;
  mixing an exact body with a mesh is refused with `boolean.operand_class_mismatch` rather than
  silently tessellating. Replacing or closing the document aborts the job and stale results never
  reach the database.
- **New `MESHREPAIR` (orientation) and `MESHREBUILD` (winding rebuild)**: closed meshes only; open
  shells are still refused with `mesh.not_closed`, and repair reports `Partial`.
- **Mesh editing**: face-region `MOVE` (with generated side walls) and `PRESSPULL` along the region
  normal, triangle-edge `MOVE`; `MESHREFINE` does a 1→4 subdivision and `MESHSMOOTH` does
  Laplacian smoothing with pinned boundaries. Every edit is one transaction and one undo step;
  derived normals/UVs/materials that no longer hold are dropped, never fabricated.
- **`MESHCAP` fills boundary loops**: planar loops are filled directly; non-planar loops are
  projected to their best-fit plane, filled and reported as `non_planar`; independent loops fill
  separately; coplanar nested loops are grouped by containment depth, an outer loop is filled with
  its direct children as holes (an annulus), and a depth-2 island is capped independently.
  Self-intersecting or degenerate loops, inconsistent winding and nested groups without a visible
  bridge are refused by name and left open.
- **Carriers**: only Linux native (`native-below`) injects the mesh kernel; Windows/macOS/Android
  and `dom-canvas` (Web, X11/XWayland) refuse with `unsupported-runtime: mesh-modeler`.

### DWG / DXF

- **Opening a DWG/DXF always returns to the canonical plan view** and never auto-enters isometric;
  a reused 3D viewport is reset too.
- **Fixed ATTRIB text being transformed twice inside scaled/rotated block references**: attributes
  are imported in the file's drawn frame and mapped back on export, dropping the bounds of a complex
  sample from about 2.7e8 to about 5.3e5 units.

### Web

- **The page carrier starts on the start page**: with no file open it shows `StartPanel` (recent
  files and recovery snapshots), and the first `OPEN` replaces the empty document in place instead
  of showing the compiled-in synthetic model.
- **`dom-canvas` now exposes standard views and orbit**: `hostProfile` declares the seven standard
  views and `orbit`, and the ViewCube views/orbit are executed by the wasm session; 2D orbit is
  still refused with the session's reason until a 3D view is selected.

### Kernel

- **The pinned `solidcore` kernel moved to upstream `f17599cb`** (727 commits): corner-unsupported
  and oversized-face errors were adapted, sweep `Status(1001)` maps to
  `solid.extrude_profile_failed`, and the fillet contract/G2 blend surfaces were resynced.
